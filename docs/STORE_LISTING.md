# Google Play store listing — draft

Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**. English is the default
language; add Hungarian under **Manage translations → Add your own translations**. Character limits
are Play's own; the counts below were checked.

## Settings outside the text

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| App category | **Food & Drink** |
| Tags | Cooking, Recipes, Meal planning (pick the closest offered) |
| Contact email | _required, shown publicly_ |
| Privacy policy URL | _the published `PRIVACY.md` text_ |
| Ads | **No ads** |
| In-app purchases | None |

## Graphics checklist

- **App icon** 512×512 PNG (32-bit, alpha) — export from `androidApp/src/main/res/mipmap-*` source.
- **Feature graphic** 1024×500 PNG/JPG — required.
- **Phone screenshots**: at least 2 (up to 8), portrait, e.g. 1080×2400. Suggested set, light theme,
  real data: Home (level + weekly challenge + recommendations) · Recipe detail with a running timer ·
  Log experience (rating) · Trophy room · Onboarding diet/allergy step · Pantry. Take them from the
  release (`qa`) build with `adb exec-out screencap -p > shot.png`. Hungarian listing: the same
  screens with the app in Hungarian.

## English

**App name** (max 30): `LevelChef: Cook & Level Up` (26)

**Short description** (max 80):
`Cook at home, earn XP and badges, and get recipes tailored to your diet.` (72)

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Turn home cooking into a game you actually want to play.

LevelChef suggests recipes that match how you eat, tracks what you cook, and rewards every meal with XP, levels and badges — so cooking more often feels like progress, not a chore.

TAILORED TO YOU
A short first-launch quiz asks about your cooking level, diet (omnivore, vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, flexitarian), allergies, favourite cuisines, spice tolerance, goals and how much time you have on weeknights. Recipe suggestions are built from your answers.

COOK WITH CONFIDENCE
• Step-by-step recipes with a servings stepper that rescales every ingredient
• Built-in step timers that keep the screen awake and ring when the time is up
• Ingredient checklist so you never miss an item
• Calories and macros for every recipe

LEVEL UP
• Earn XP for every meal you cook and climb from Kitchen Novice upwards
• A new weekly challenge every Monday
• Streaks and badges for cooking regularly, trying new ingredients and more
• Rate each meal and note what you'd do better next time

YOUR KITCHEN, YOUR DATA
• A pantry of the ingredients you've tried, with nutrition values
• Your cooking history stays on your phone — no account, no ads
• Delete everything at any time from Settings

Light and dark theme, English and Hungarian.

Good to know: recipe suggestions are generated by AI (Google Gemini) and can contain mistakes. LevelChef asks it to respect your diet and allergies, but always check the ingredients yourself — the app is not medical or nutritional advice.
```
