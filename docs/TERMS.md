# Terms of Use — draft

> **Status: draft for the maintainer to fill in and review before publishing.** Placeholders are in
> _italics_. Not legal advice — for a commercial or widely distributed app, have it checked by a
> lawyer. Publish it at a public URL next to the privacy policy ([`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md)) and
> link both from the store listing and from Settings.

## How the three documents relate

| Document | Required? | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Privacy policy** (`PRIVACY.md`) | **Yes** — Google Play requires a public URL for every app, and especially for one handling health-related data (diet, allergies) | What data the app collects, where it goes, how to delete it |
| **Data safety form** | **Yes** — filled in inside Play Console, shown on the store page | Google's structured summary of the same facts; **must match** the privacy policy, or the app can be rejected/removed |
| **Terms of use** (this file) | **No**, not required by Google Play | The rules for using the app and the limits of your responsibility — mainly protects *you*: AI recipes may be wrong, allergen info is not a guarantee |

So the terms of use are independent of the Data safety form; the privacy policy and the Data safety
form must say the same thing.
